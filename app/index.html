Roadmap to the platinum

Built only from the official trophy descriptions. GTA VI released 19 November 2026 and no verified step-by-step guide exists yet, so this guide invents no locations, NPCs or step sequences — each trophy's guide says what the description asks and how to approach it in general terms, with its source cited.

  1. Stage 1 — Story, Chapters 1–4. Play the story in order: opening mission Gone Fishing, then Chapters 1, 2, 3 and 4, with the named missions (Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory) along the way. Every one of these trophies is awarded on completion and cannot be missed.
  2. Stage 2 — Contact & stranger strands. Finish the final mission for each contact — Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone — and complete 8 stranger mission strands across Leonida. Strands stay open once unlocked, so none is missable.
  3. Stage 3 — Activities & counts. Sample all 9 activity types, then work the counted trophies: 250 stunts, 42 Shooting Range gold medals, 20 race wins, 14 Top Fun drone-race wins, 50 challenges, 5 Combat Focus takedowns in one use, and the wanted-level, robbery and money trophies. Everything here is retryable.
  4. Stage 4 — Collectibles & exploration. 3 card sets, WAiNK It at 100 locations, 18 meditation spots, 100 vehicles discovered, 10 registered to Scooter Bros, 8 garages bought, and all 52 areas of Leonida explored. Sweep region by region; nothing closes.
  5. Stage 5 — Finale & 100%. Complete the final mission (gold), then mop up anything left until the game reads 100% completion (gold). The platinum, Vice Got a Price!, unlocks automatically with it.

Missable trophies: none. No official description states a one-time condition. Story trophies unlock on completion; every activity, collectible and counted trophy can be attempted again after it unlocks. If a verified guide later shows a one-time condition, this note will be corrected — until then, nothing here is flagged missable.

Sources: every trophy's name, description and grade are as PlayStation publishes them, via Exophase — Grand Theft Auto VI trophies (PSN) and PSNProfiles, 2026-11-18. Each guide is written for this page from the official description; no Rockstar artwork or logo is used.
